How I work

The player-coach model

I'm not a leader who hands down a brief and disappears into slide decks. I sit with the team — in Figma, in the prototype, in the prompt window — and I ship. Across regulated finance, global FMCG, public broadcasting and tier-one agencies, I've learned the fastest way to move a large organisation is to combine board-level fluency with hands-on craft: token systems I help architect myself, AI workflows I test before I ask a team to adopt them, service blueprints I still sketch on a whiteboard.

That's the model I bring to every engagement: strategic enough to set direction and win the room, technical enough to stay in the build with product, design and engineering — using whatever the latest tool demands, from design-token pipelines to generative AI prototyping.
