Why this role

Three things I would bring on day one

01

Safe by design, practised not quoted

At Ford I set the information hierarchy for a cabin system governed by driver cognitive load, with safety-critical validation across four hardware platforms. At HSBC I mapped the trust and control boundaries for generative AI inside a regulated bank. Both are the same discipline: designing for someone who can be harmed by a careless interface, and building the guardrails in rather than auditing them on afterwards.

02

Inclusive by default

The Bedrock design system enforced WCAG 2.1 AA at the atomic component layer, so accessibility was a property of the components rather than a report at the end of a project. Teams could not accidentally ship an inaccessible pattern. That is the only version of inclusive design that survives contact with 120+ product teams.

03

Physical and digital as one brief

Connected wearables, retail activations, hardware prototypes, visitor experiences and multi-screen cabins. I have spent most of my career where an object and a screen have to make sense as a single experience, including the parts nobody demos: the packaging, the first ten seconds, and what happens when the connection drops.

The honest part

What I have not done

I have not designed a product for children. There is no point dressing up adjacent work as though I have, and you would see through it in a first conversation anyway.

What I would bring instead is the discipline underneath it. Designing for someone who cannot be expected to read the instructions, and who is put at risk when the interface is careless, is the Ford brief. Holding a duty of care to a mass household audience is the BBC brief. Building guardrails into a system so that people cannot wander somewhere harmful is the HSBC brief. Making a physical object and a screen feel like one thing is most of the rest of this page.

Safe-by-design and wellbeing-by-design are the right frames for digital play, and they are the frames I have worked in elsewhere under different names. The domain knowledge about how children actually play, I would expect to learn from the people who already have it, quickly and with a lot of questions.
