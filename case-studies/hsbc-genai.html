GenAI & Innovation Transformation

Led design thinking and hands-on prototyping to bring safe, human-in-the-loop GenAI to 14,000+ global staff.

Role

Innovation & AI Manager (Player-Coach)

Focus

Conversational AI adoption inside a highly regulated global bank

Timeframe

2025–2026 · Regulated Finance

The Challenge

Global Value Streams suffered from legacy system friction, strict regulatory hurdles and a risk-averse operational culture. HSBC needed to securely adopt internal Generative AI to unlock efficiency without risking data leaks or compliance failures.

The Approach

  • Use-case evaluation. Architected a UX framework to identify duplication and prioritise workstreams for internal GenAI tools, ensuring high trust and clear system states.
  • Prompt-engineering training. Built and delivered training frameworks, channels and workshops to upskill cross-functional design, product and engineering teams in day-to-day AI use.
  • Process optimisation. Blueprinted complex banking workflows to pinpoint high-value automation nodes, replacing legacy friction with conversational interfaces.

The Impact

  • De-risked enterprise AI. Established compliant UX paradigms that met stringent financial regulations, clearing the path for scaled GenAI deployment.
  • Operational velocity. Drastically reduced time-to-output for internal analytical and operational workflows through optimised AI orchestration.
  • Experience-led culture. Turned risk-averse stakeholders into active AI collaborators — training reached 14,000+ global staff.
