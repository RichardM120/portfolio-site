Product Innovation — R7 Speaker

Built the “Hello World” prototype that secured 100% executive sign-off in Korea for Samsung's Radiant360 R7 speaker.

Role

Project Director

Focus

Physical-digital prototyping to win executive buy-in for a new hardware category

Timeframe

Early career · Consumer Electronics · Product Innovation

The Challenge

Samsung Europe needed to validate and launch a radically new hardware form factor — the R7 360° omnidirectional speaker — for Western markets. The hurdle was translating a conceptual audio idea into a tangible experience that could win production buy-in from senior leadership in Korea.

The Approach
  • Hardware-software synthesis. Orchestrated a cross-continental bridge between European design strategy and Korean technical development to build a working prototype.
  • Phygital prototyping. Led the team building functional hardware prototypes integrating custom physical speaker controls with a companion mobile app.
  • Executive curation. Engineered a friction-free live demonstration built specifically to showcase the hardware-software interaction during executive review.
The Impact
  • 100% executive buy-in. The live demonstration secured full development funding and production sign-off from Samsung's global executive team in Korea.
  • Global launch. The prototype was the direct catalyst for full-scale manufacturing and the commercial release of the Samsung Radiant360 R7.
  • Strategic influence. Set a precedent that localised Western design innovation could shape Samsung's global product pipeline.
