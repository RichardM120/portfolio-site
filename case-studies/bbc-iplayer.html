BBC iPlayer — Launch Release 1

Managed design delivery on BBC iPlayer V1 — redefining how the UK consumes content and setting the benchmark for public-service VOD.

Role

UX Design Project Manager

Focus

Delivering V1 of BBC iPlayer at the dawn of streaming

Timeframe

2006–2011 · Public Broadcasting · VOD

The Challenge

The BBC needed to move a massive, multi-generational broadcast audience from traditional television to a digital-first, on-demand model — at a time when streaming technology was in its infancy.

The Approach
  • Foundational delivery. Managed development of the iPlayer platform, ensuring the underlying architecture could handle national-scale traffic.
  • User-centric streaming. Prioritised seamless navigation and high-performance playback, bridging broadcast quality with a modern digital interface.
The Impact
  • Platform foundation. Played a critical role delivering iPlayer, which redefined how UK audiences consume content.
  • Digital adoption. Accelerated the BBC's shift to digital-first, setting the benchmark for public-service broadcasting worldwide.
