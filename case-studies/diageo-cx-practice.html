Design Systems & CX Diageo

Global CX/UX Practice

Founded Diageo's global CX/UX practice from a 4-person team, standardising design across 40+ agencies and shifting UX from execution to strategy.

Role

Global Head of CX/UX (8-year tenure, founder)

Focus

Built Diageo's centralised UX capability from foundational to best-in-class

Timeframe

2018-2025 · Global FMCG · Practice Leadership

The Challenge

Diageo's decentralised ecosystem lacked standardised UX processes, causing inconsistent e-commerce experiences and brand risk. The business needed to scale design capability across 40+ global agencies while moving UX upstream, from downstream execution to a strategic driver.

The Approach

  • The UX playbook. Authored an end-to-end Double Diamond framework, embedding design principles and consumer research into the earliest product phases.
  • Tool centralisation. Standardised the global design stack, moving 40+ agencies onto centralised instances of Figma, Miro and Mural.
  • Accessibility by design. Partnered with WCAG consultants to embed strict accessibility standards, reducing defects and protecting brand equity.
  • AI piloting, with guardrails. Evaluated GenAI for automated alt-text, then paused rollout after documenting a 40% failure rate, protecting compliance over speed.

The Impact

  • Accelerated maturity. Shifted Diageo's UX adoption curve to match best-in-class market competitors.
  • Operational efficiency. Global standardisation cut cross-agency defects and accelerated time-to-market.
  • Brand protection. Institutionalised inclusive design principles, protecting global brand equity and mitigating legal risk.
