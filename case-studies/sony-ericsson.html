Mobile Colour LCD Screen Pioneers

Brokered the 2002 Nokia contract that put Sony Ericsson display technology into the iconic Nokia N95.

Role

Technical Project Lead / Global Liaison

Focus

Cross-continental R&D bridging Japanese display technology with global handset brands

Timeframe

Early career · Consumer Electronics · Display R&D

The Challenge

The mobile industry's most explosive growth period demanded navigating the rapid evolution of colour LCD, reflective displays and LED backlighting — alongside multi-million-dollar contract negotiation across continents.

The Approach

  • R&D bridge. Served as technical liaison between Sony R&D/manufacturing teams in Japan and global clients including Nokia, Motorola and Siemens.
  • Engineering collaboration. Facilitated deep-dive workshops between Japanese scientists, display engineers and Western engineering teams to align roadmaps with market-leading specs.
  • Global execution. Managed cross-continental delivery, running production workshops in Helsinki, Miami and Germany to fold local market needs into global hardware standards.

The Impact

  • Flagship success. Negotiated the landmark 2002 Nokia contract, directly enabling the Nokia N95's development and global launch.
  • Global footprint. Established Sony Ericsson as a dominant display supplier through deep partnerships with Motorola and Siemens.
  • Technical benchmarking. Bridged raw Japanese R&D capability with the final product, setting the standard for visual quality through the feature-phone-to-smartphone era.
