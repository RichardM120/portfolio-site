Global CX & D2C Measurement Framework

Closed the gap between granular site friction and brand-health tracking with one honeycomb measurement taxonomy across five core journeys.

Role

Global Experience Design, Ecommerce & DTC Lead

Focus

One measurement language across a multi-brand digital estate

Timeframe

2018–2025 · Global FMCG · DesignOps

The Challenge

Diageo's multi-brand digital estate lacked standardised CX metrics, leaving a gap between granular site friction (rage clicks) and high-level brand-health tracking.

The Approach

  • Honeycomb taxonomy. Unified UX, CX and BX across five core journeys: Shop, Learn, Explore, Visit, Support.
  • Double Diamond KPI mapping. Linked Discovery metrics (SUS, task-success) directly to Build/Release analytics (CSAT, CES, NPS, CVR).
  • Three-tier model. Structured data into clear Operational (usability), Tactical (validation) and Strategic (experience) tiers.

The Impact

  • Enterprise alignment. Standardised a single CX language across global Product, Marketing and Engineering teams.
  • Accelerated insight. Replaced slow, committee-led analysis with agile, data-backed product backlogs.
  • Repeatable blueprint. Established a framework to validate every feature release against conversion metrics.
